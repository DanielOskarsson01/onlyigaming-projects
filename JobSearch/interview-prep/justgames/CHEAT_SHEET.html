JustGames CMO — On-Call Cheat Sheet

Keep this open during the Nathan call. One page.

Opening — 60-90 sec when asked "tell me about yourself"

Founding team, MrGreen (7 → 250, NASDAQ). CMO/CPO/COO ComeOn (1 → 100+ marketing org, NASDAQ, Best Operator 4 yrs running). Built Coinhero from zero — crypto-native operator. 0-to-1 specialist. Full-stack. Done multi-market launches twice.

Close with:

"What I'd want to understand on this call is whether the role and the company are set up to use that experience — particularly around licensing, channel access, and timeline."

Top 3 fit angles (pick 2-3 to lead with)

  1. 0-to-1 founding-team CMO, twice — MrGreen and ComeOn
  2. Built marketing function from 1 → 100+ people — most CMOs scale, you build
  3. Crypto-native operator (Coinhero) — most iGaming CMOs haven't operated crypto rails

Other ammo: full-stack ownership (marketing + CRM + product + ops + BI), ambassador-led launch experience, multi-market launches (20+ markets, 4+ country-management roles).

Sharpest questions (ask 5-7, in order)

  1. "How did Igor's team come to engage Talent SDK? Your background is gaming/AI/defense — curious how this role landed with you."
  2. "What's the legal entity name and jurisdiction of incorporation?"
  3. "Of the four founders, who's run a regulated betting operation before? How is product / engineering staffed below Peter?"
  4. "How is the $3.5M structured — pre-seed, seed, SAFE? Who's the lead investor? What's the Series A target?"
  5. "Why hire a CMO 4-6 weeks before launch rather than 4-6 months ago? Who's been doing marketing until now?"
  6. "What does Igor expect the CMO to own on day one — brand + growth only, or also CRM, retention, ops, analytics?"
  7. "Comp structure — base, equity, cash bonus, token allocation if there's a token? OTE range?"
  8. (If room) "What's the licensing strategy for EU, AU, Brazil — protocol with third-party frontends or own licensed frontend?"
  9. (Close) "What's the process from here, timeline, who I'd meet at each stage?"

Comp range — signal, don't negotiate

Base $200-280K + 2-3% equity + token allocation if exists + severance + double-trigger acceleration.

If Nathan deflects:

"Understood. Can you tell me even a range so we don't waste each other's time?"

  3. Hiring CMO 4-6 weeks pre-launch = reversed sequencing
  4. Recruiter is gaming/AI/defense, not iGaming = founder under-networking signal
  5. Founders are poker-world, not betting-platform-world (Igor + Dan poker pros; Tom + Peter unverifiable)

Post-call score — immediate, 60 seconds
