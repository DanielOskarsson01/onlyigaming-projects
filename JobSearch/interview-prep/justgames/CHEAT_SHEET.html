JustGames CMO — On-Call Cheat Sheet

Keep this open during the Nathan call. One page.

Opening — 60-90 sec when asked "tell me about yourself"

Founding team, MrGreen (7 → 250, NASDAQ). CMO/CPO/COO ComeOn (1 → 100+ marketing org, NASDAQ, Best Operator 4 yrs running). Built Coinhero from zero — crypto-native operator. 0-to-1 specialist. Full-stack. Done multi-market launches twice.

Close with:

"What I'd want to understand on this call is whether the role and the company are set up to use that experience — particularly around licensing, channel access, and timeline."

Top 3 fit angles (pick 2-3 to lead with)

  1. 0-to-1 founding-team CMO, twice — MrGreen and ComeOn
  2. Built marketing function from 1 → 100+ people — most CMOs scale, you build
  3. Crypto-native operator (Coinhero) — most iGaming CMOs haven't operated crypto rails

Other ammo: full-stack ownership (marketing + CRM + product + ops + BI), ambassador-led launch experience, multi-market launches (20+ markets, 4+ country-management roles).

Questions — four areas, in this order

Goal: find out what JustGames actually IS. If Nathan can answer A and B with specifics, this is real.

A. PRODUCT — what am I selling, to whom?

  1. "Walk me through the product — is JustGames a B2C sportsbook on its own protocol, a B2B infrastructure layer operators plug into, or both — protocol + flagship frontend?"
  2. "Who's the customer the CMO acquires — end-users, operators, or developer teams building frontends?"
  3. "Is there a thought about JustGames running its own branded B2C sportsbook on top of the protocol, or strictly to enable other operators?"

B. LICENSING — can the customer legally touch it?

  1. "Geographic strategy — which countries on day one, which geo-blocked? Licensed markets, gray markets, or both with different stacks?"
  2. "B2B side — selling to licensed operators (MGA, UKGC, ADM, Brazilian Bets, Ontario) or gray-market crypto operators?"
  3. "Can the product be licensed at all? Is the protocol itself MGA Class 2 / Brazilian Bets eligible, or deliberately outside licensing — let frontends carry licenses?"
  4. "What does legal say about Brazil specifically — going through Bets licensing or geo-blocking at launch?"

C. AMBASSADORS — who and for what?

  1. "Who are the ambassadors — poker, crypto/Web3, sports, or a mix?"
  2. "What's the ambassador's actual role — B2C signups, credibility for operator sales, or audience distribution? Phil Hellmuth for B2C trial is completely different from using him at a B2B conference."
  3. "If this is B2B, what would an ambassador actually do? Operators don't buy infrastructure from poker pros — they buy from a sales team with certifications and references."
  4. "Equity holders, paid spokespeople, or token allocation? Deal structure shapes how aggressively I can ask them to work."

D. THE $1M BUDGET — what does Igor see it spent on?

  1. "What does Igor see the $1M spent on — conferences and industry sales, influencer deals, crypto KOLs and points, paid acquisition, cold outbound to operators?"
  2. "What's the rough split between B2B sales motion (conferences, trade press, cold outbound) vs B2C acquisition (paid, affiliates, ambassadors)? $1M is plenty for one, tight for both."
  3. "Is $1M for pre-launch awareness only, or first 6 months of operating spend?"
  4. "What channels are off the table given the regulatory posture? Google/Meta closed for unlicensed; sports sponsorships need an operator license."

Close-out (90 sec)

  1. "Legal entity name and jurisdiction?"
  2. "$3.5M structure, lead investor, Series A milestone?"
  3. "Process from here, timeline, who I'd meet?"
  4. "What would make a candidate stand out to Igor specifically?"
