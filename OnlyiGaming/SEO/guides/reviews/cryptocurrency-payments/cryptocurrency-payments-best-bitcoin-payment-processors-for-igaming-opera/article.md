## Common Questions

### What's the difference between crypto-native and bolt-on processors?

Crypto-native platforms like SOFTSWISS built their entire architecture around cryptocurrency processing. ### How do FX conversion costs affect crypto payment economics?

FX markups on crypto-to-fiat conversion often exceed published fee structures by 1-3%. Always request written FX spreads for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT before signing.

### Can crypto payment processors handle Brazil's 2026 licensed operator restrictions?

EveryMatrix and GR8 Tech offer platform-level geo-fencing that disables crypto features for Brazilian players while maintaining full functionality elsewhere. SOFTSWISS primarily serves unregulated markets and lacks granular compliance tools for multi-jurisdiction operators. Bolt-on processors typically require operators to handle geo-fencing through their main platform, creating compliance gaps when crypto and fiat regulations differ.

### Which cryptocurrencies matter most for igaming operations?

Bitcoin and Ethereum handle 70%+ of crypto gambling volume globally. USDT provides stability for markets with currency volatility. Beyond these three, altcoin relevance varies by player geography - Eastern Europeans use Bitcoin heavily, Asians prefer newer altcoins, Brazilians favor USDT. Focus on proven volume over comprehensive altcoin lists unless your players specifically demand newer tokens.

### How long does crypto payment processor integration typically take?

Native crypto platforms like SOFTSWISS require 2-4 weeks for full integration including wallet setup, compliance configuration, and testing. Bolt-on processors like NOWPayments can integrate in 3-5 days but offer limited customization. Hybrid solutions like GR8 Tech typically need 3-6 weeks for complete fiat-crypto unification. Budget additional time for compliance setup if operating in multiple jurisdictions.

### What security considerations apply specifically to crypto payment processing?

Hot wallet exposure creates the primary security risk - processors should maintain minimal hot wallet balances with cold storage for larger amounts. SOFTSWISS uses multi-signature wallets with geographic key distribution. Ask about insurance coverage for hot wallet losses, withdrawal approval limits, and cold storage procedures. Verify that your processor maintains SOC 2 compliance and provides wallet security audits.
