## Common Questions

### What is a modular casino platform and why should I care?

A modular casino platform separates core functions like player management, game aggregation, payments, and bonus engines into independent modules that can be licensed, deployed, and upgraded separately. This matters because you can optimize each component independently, switch underperforming modules without platform migration, and adapt to regulatory changes faster than monolithic setups.

### How is a modular casino platform different from a monolithic platform?

Monolithic platforms bundle all functionality into a single system with shared databases and tightly coupled components. If you need to change payment processing, you might affect game aggregation. Modular platforms use APIs to connect independent modules - you can upgrade payments without touching anything else. Brazilian operators have experienced challenges, with monolithic platforms reportedly requiring significant time for tax compliance changes while modular setups have completed updates in shorter timeframes.

Switch when you're hitting integration bottlenecks, struggling with vendor lock-in, or facing regulatory deadlines your current platform can't meet quickly. If you need several months for simple integrations, can't negotiate component-level pricing, or your platform vendor controls your roadmap completely, modular architecture will give you more control.
