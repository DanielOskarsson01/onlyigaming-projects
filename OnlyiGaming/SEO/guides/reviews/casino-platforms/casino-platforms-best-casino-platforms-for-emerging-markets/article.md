**EveryMatrix's PaymentIQ** provides currency routing capabilities but typically requires operators to manage hedging relationships separately. **What to verify before signing:** Understand exactly who bears currency risk during the conversion process and what hedging options the platform provides. Request specific examples of how they handle major currency movements.

**Trap question for vendors:** "How do you handle currency conversion when [local currency] moves 5% in a week - does the operator absorb that or do you?" Their answer reveals whether they understand emerging market currency risk.
